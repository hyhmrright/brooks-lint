Reports follow evidence / 报告以证据为准

A finding states the symptom, source principle, concrete consequence and scoped remedy. A book citation supplies a diagnostic lens, not proof that a defect exists. Unknown obligations stay explicit; a healthy score is not a safety certificate.

每条发现说明症状、原则来源、具体后果和修复范围。书籍引用不等于缺陷证据；未知条件须明确保留，健康分不代表安全认证。

Health derives scores from one ledger. Delivery checks bind the supplied report bytes, not a model's diagnosis or later display. Sweep uses authorized edits and bounded repair rounds, reporting unresolved work at its stop boundary.
