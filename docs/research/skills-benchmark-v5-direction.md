# Project direction after the v4 comparison

Status: prospective offline design, 2026-11-13. Historical runs, adjudications and
frozen prompts remain evidence of their original versions. New instructions are
candidates for testing, not demonstrated improvements.

## Product claim and scope

Brooks-lint provides portable, evidence-led engineering review procedures informed
by the source books. It standardizes finding structure, scope, uncertainty,
priorities and scoring. It does not yet have evidence of a general diagnostic
advantage over the selected model with a generic checklist. V4 tied all three
arms on frozen labels and expected-issue recall; skill guidance cost more tokens
and had two final-report delivery failures. Evidence credits improved in some
modes and declined in others. Do not market a composite score as a probability
of correctness, a release certificate, or an objective measure of all code quality.

| Entry point | Intended user outcome | Success evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| brooks-review | Actionable findings on a change with its governing contract | Supported causal diagnosis, relevant witness, severity and scoped remedy |
| brooks-audit | Explain dependency structure and violated boundaries | Verified edges and actual boundary obligations; unknown binding remains explicit |
| brooks-debt | Prioritize established maintenance burden | Shared decision ownership, change propagation and conditional priority |
| brooks-test | Identify protection gaps and harmful test coupling | Behavioral obligation, paired tests/mutations and correct inventory scope |
| brooks-health | Summarize assessed dimensions consistently | Evidence-backed statuses, one reconciled ledger, exact verified report |
| brooks-sweep | Convenient bounded orchestration of the other five skills | Preserved user work, authorized edits, actual verification, bounded stop and residuals |

The first five are diagnostic entry points. Sweep stays available as opt-in
orchestration because that is its user purpose; it is not a sixth independent
diagnostic algorithm. Its candidate patches need separate execution evidence.
Passing a schema or listing five phases does not establish autonomous repair.
The runtime pack remains experimental research with exposed case history; do not
merge its claims into shipped skill effectiveness or claim independent validation.

## Diagnosis and proposed changes

- **Symptom:** successful health receipts were followed by edited final reports.
  **Source:** v4 BV4-31/32 C and the finalizer delivery contract.
  **Consequence:** correct tool arithmetic did not bind the delivered artifact.
  **Remedy:** make draft, reconciliation, finalization and unchanged delivery
  explicit states; compare the outgoing content where a host supports it. Preserve
  the observed model output in evaluation, including failures; do not replace it
  with a tool result to manufacture model adherence.
- **Symptom:** unsupported architecture scores and contradictory debt ledgers.
  **Source:** v4 BV4-16/17/18 C and scope/assessment rules.
  **Consequence:** numerical validity masks unsupported semantic conclusions.
  **Remedy:** map scope, inspected inputs, governing obligation and blockers;
  reconcile every scored prose finding with its owning ledger entry before
  finalization. A deterministic calculator cannot validate diagnosis truth.
- **Symptom:** test assessment was withheld despite a complete supplied inventory
  and explicit contract. **Source:** v4 BV4-31 C.
  **Consequence:** general uncertainty rules suppress an assessable local result.
  **Remedy:** distinguish missing broader history from a fact required for the
  declared local assessment, and inspect explicit behavior protection obligations.

These changes preserve risk codes, score weights, trigger boundaries and mode
steps. They address observed failure mechanisms through general procedures;
real retesting is still required to measure adherence.

## Evaluation direction and decisions

Keep the exposed v4 cases as regression evidence. Prepare new development cases
across the retained modes with operational rubrics, distinct mechanisms,
positive/protected/insufficient-information counterparts and offline witnesses.
Use repeated A/B/C comparisons; case families, not repeated answers alone, are
the generalization unit. Report exact-report retention, ledger truth, diagnostic
evidence, tool failures and separately verified patch behavior as distinct outcomes.
Do not collapse these into a label-only leaderboard. Report tokens and missing
usage alongside outcomes, without presenting observed tokens as billed dollars.

Self-authored cases and agents from the same workflow are not independent
holdouts. Semantic judgments are same-workflow, AI-assisted judgments, not
independent human review. Freeze cases, grading policy, prompts and budget before any real run;
record author exposure. External blind cases remain an evidence limitation, not
a blocker on autonomous offline preparation. A saturated label metric calls for
new mechanisms and task outcomes; repeated runs estimate variability but do not
make known examples independent. Never change an old rubric or answer to improve
a retrospective result, and retain supported unexpected findings as oracle gaps.

A candidate skill revision earns continuation when its intended task outcomes
improve without increased unsupported claims or workflow failures. With these
small development sets, report descriptive paired counts and uncertainty rather
than universal efficacy. If C continues to match B while costing more, simplify
that skill's instructions and narrow the claim; if orchestration cannot preserve
work or stop reliably, withhold autonomous-repair claims and keep it bounded.
Publication and a larger live campaign are separate decisions from this offline
preparation. The frozen execution document specifies model, effort, submissions,
time, token admission and stop rules; no live call is authorized by this document.
