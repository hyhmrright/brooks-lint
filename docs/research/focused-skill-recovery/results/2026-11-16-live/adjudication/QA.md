## Judgment and limits

The two changed judgments are supported by the supplied contract and reproduced local behavior. Infinity violates the explicit integer postcondition for an accepted all-digit input without requiring a decision about signed/exponent grammar or a future representation. Changing the asserted expected value is behaviorally equivalent for the narrow local obligation that a failed assertion must escape; it is not evidence about a broader parser contract.

No actionable inconsistency found. This is targeted same-workflow agent QA, not independent human validation or a new regrading of all responses. The small observed advantage does not establish population-level effectiveness. Release preparation still requires the bounded integration candidate, full release audit, exact-commit CI and explicit publication authorization described in the decision.
