The test inventory has one **defect: T5 Coverage Illusion**. The contract requires the test to check that numeric strings return an integer, that other strings are rejected, and that a test does not suppress its own failed assertion. The outer `try/catch` in `existing.test.mjs` catches assertion failures and lets the test complete successfully. Because the supplied test is the complete inventory, no other supplied test protects these behaviors.
