A partition ownership epoch changes on reassignment. A delayed completion from a revoked epoch must not change either the materialized total or its checkpoint. Each current-epoch completion atomically applies its delta and checkpoint. The fixture is an application-owned checkpoint sink, not a model of Kafka broker internals. Each batch supplies ascending offsets, but its callbacks can complete in any order; the batch buffer drains only its contiguous ready prefix. There is at most one batch per epoch.

Batch handles survive reassignment unless an external scheduler contract explicitly drains them. Duplicate completion callbacks for a batch index must not apply its effect twice. A new epoch begins its batch after the last offset reserved to the old epoch; offsets therefore remain globally increasing at dispatch.
