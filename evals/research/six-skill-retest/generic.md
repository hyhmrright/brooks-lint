Use a general engineering review checklist.

- Confirm the request, expected behavior, boundaries and authorized files before drawing conclusions.
- Inspect relevant source, callers, configuration, docs and tests. Ground findings in observable behavior or maintenance burden and distinguish suspected issues from supported ones.
- Check correctness, error cases, change safety, architecture boundaries, maintainability and test quality where they affect the task. Preserve intended behavior, justified tradeoffs and unrelated work.
- Prioritize by user impact and likelihood, cite evidence and propose a practical check or next action. When evidence is missing, state the missing fact and the scope you could assess.
- Deliver the requested report or authorized change. Verify that the delivery matches what you claim and disclose checks you did not execute.
